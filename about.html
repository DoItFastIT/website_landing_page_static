At Do It Fast IT, we don't just build tools; we build the bridge between your creativity and your audience. Our mission is to simplify social media management so you can focus on what matters most — creating.

Our Story

Founded in 2025 by the MAS Hunter Team, Do It Fast IT was born out of a simple observation: managing social media is becoming too complex. Between scheduling, engaging, and analyzing data across dozens of platforms, creators were losing their most valuable asset — time.

We set out to build a unified command center that feels as fast as it is powerful. A place where data is clear, scheduling is seamless, and engagement is effortless.

Our Core Values
