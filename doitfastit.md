## 2. What the Company Does

Do It Fast IT is a **Social Media Management Platform**. It allows clients to connect all their social media accounts to one central web-based workspace. From that single dashboard, users can:

- **Connect** all social media accounts in one place
- **Schedule & Publish** content to multiple platforms simultaneously
- **Engage** — reply to comments and manage DMs from a unified inbox
- **Analyze** — track post performance, follower growth, and engagement metrics in real time
- **Manage** everything without switching between apps or platforms

The platform is designed to save time, reduce complexity, and help users build an authentic online following faster.

## 8. Core Service Features (Product Breakdown)

### 🔗 Connect
Link all social media platforms through a single OAuth-based integration. One account, all platforms connected within seconds.

### 📅 Schedule & Publish
Plan a content calendar and auto-publish posts to multiple social media platforms at a set date and time — without manual intervention on each platform.

### 💬 Unified Inbox / Engage
Centralizes all incoming comments, replies, mentions, and DMs from every connected platform into one inbox. The user responds from the same place without logging into individual apps.

### 📊 Analytics & Performance Tracking
Provides real-time data on post reach, impressions, likes, comments, follower growth, and engagement rate across all platforms from a single dashboard view.
